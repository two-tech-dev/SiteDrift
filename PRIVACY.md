# SiteDrift Privacy Policy

## What We Collect

SiteDrift does not collect any personally identifiable information. The only data collected is described below.

## Crowd Bypass

When **Crowd Bypass** is enabled (Options → "Give and take the destinations of unbypassable shorteners"), the extension occasionally sends requests to our server:

- When you visit a known unbypassable shortener, the URL is sent to check if someone has already reported its destination.
- When you complete the visit, the URL and destination are sent to be recorded for future visitors.

To prevent abuse, a **hashed version** of your IP address is stored alongside each submission. Only 2Tech Studio developers have access to this database. We never share this data with third parties.

We use Cloudflare to protect our server. Their [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) applies to you as an end user.
